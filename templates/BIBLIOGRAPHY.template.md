# BIBLIOGRAPHY.md — Reference Base (Template)

> **Adopter notes.** This template is an APA 7-formatted skeleton you populate as your programme grows. The reverse contract is: every entry below is referenced at least once in your framework artefacts; entries that lose their load-bearing role are deleted in the next pass. The seed entries are anchor citations the framework's design itself relies on; you should retain them. Add discipline-specific anchor citations under the relevant headings. Italicised paragraphs are guidance and should be deleted from the final populated file. Switch to your discipline's preferred citation format if APA 7 is not the right choice (per `REQUIREMENTS.md` R5).

This is the consolidated reference base on which this project's instantiation of Stoa leans. References are grouped thematically and formatted to APA 7 (American Psychological Association, 2020) by default; adopters in disciplines with different citation conventions adapt accordingly. Verification of `[verify]`-flagged entries is required before any external publication of derivative work.

## Verification policy

Entries marked *[verify]* are highly likely to be correct as cited but include at least one element — exact edition year, URL stability, page range, or position-statement title — that should be confirmed by an authoritative reference-management process before any external publication of derivative work. The verification pass is non-negotiable for any artefact that will be cited in peer-reviewed scholarship.

## Update policy

Amend this bibliography whenever a framework artefact adds a load-bearing citation. Record amendments in `STATE.md` under *Bibliography revisions*. Removed entries — those that lose their load-bearing role through a rewrite — are deleted in the same pass to keep the reverse contract intact.
