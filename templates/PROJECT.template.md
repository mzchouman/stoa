## The Framework — Stoa

The integrated operating model under which this programme runs is *Stoa*. Every other artefact in this project — `REQUIREMENTS.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `STATE.md`, `OPERATING-MODEL.md`, `DESIGN-RATIONALE.md`, `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`, the Academic Vern `SKILL.md`, the per-paper `{n}-CONTEXT.md` skeletons, and the bootstrap prompt — operates inside Stoa and refers to it by name.

### What Stoa is

Stoa is a layered configuration of durable file artefacts, workflow protocols, persona-discipline standards, and a non-overridable audit gate, which together produce outputs of doctoral and Q1-tier-publishable quality across long-running research programmes that span months to years. It is engineered to satisfy the requirements stated in `REQUIREMENTS.md`, defended as a structured design exercise in `DESIGN-RATIONALE.md`, and operationalised through the loop and adaptations described in `OPERATING-MODEL.md`.

Stoa stacks three altitudes. At the programme altitude, Stoa implements a spec-driven artefact-spine discipline translated for academic publishing. At the paper altitude, Stoa composes with a discipline-specific drafting bundle for drafting mechanics — manuscript structure, methods boilerplate, results presentation. At the sentence altitude, Stoa enforces the evidentiary discipline of the Academic Vern persona. The three altitudes are mutually compatible because each operates on a different unit of work.

Stoa is not a writing tutor, not a journal selector, not a citation manager, and not a peer reviewer in the institutional sense. It does not replace supervisor review, IRB processes, journal editorial decisions, or the candidate's own intellectual labour. Its value lies in raising the floor of what is produced — by encoding the standards a doctoral committee or a Q1-tier reviewer would expect and by enforcing them through a non-overridable gate before any external submission.

### What this means for adopters

A programme operating under Stoa commits to the following. Maintain a persistent four-artefact spine (`PROJECT.md`, `REQUIREMENTS.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `STATE.md`) plus the `OPERATING-MODEL.md` playbook, all maintained as living files at the project root. Run each output through the four-step loop (discuss-phase, plan-phase, execute-phase, audit-milestone) with a per-output context-research-plan artefact triple. Respect the non-overridability of the audit gate. Apply the Academic Vern evidentiary discipline at the sentence level. Comply with the integrity provisions in `REQUIREMENTS.md` R8 — sole intellectual authorship, AI disclosure compliant with target-journal policy, and absolute disqualification of fabrication or undisclosed self-plagiarism. Maintain cross-output coherence per R12 and the versioning convention in R13.

### Provenance

Stoa draws on the broader spec-driven development tradition in software engineering and on the academic writing pedagogy and methodological-transparency literatures in management research. It composes with discipline-specific drafting bundles where they exist. Provenance is documented in full at `PROVENANCE.md` in the public-release directory.
