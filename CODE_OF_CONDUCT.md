# Code of Conduct

Stoa is a piece of methodological infrastructure intended for adoption by doctoral candidates and researchers across many disciplines, institutions, and countries. The community surrounding it should reflect the standards of respectful, evidence-based scholarly engagement that the framework itself encodes. This document sets out those standards.

## Our commitment

The maintainer commits, and asks contributors and adopters to commit, to making participation in the framework's community a respectful, productive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Standards we hold

Participants in this community are expected to demonstrate behaviour that contributes to a respectful and intellectually serious environment. Examples of behaviour that meets the standard include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language, particularly when engaging with adopters who are early in their doctoral journey or who are working in their second or third language.
- Engaging substantively with the framework's design choices and limitations rather than dismissing them.
- Citing evidence when proposing changes or critiquing existing artefacts; the framework's own evidentiary discipline applies to discussion about it.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully when offered, and offering constructive criticism kindly when warranted.
- Focusing on what is best for the community of adopters, not on what is most convenient for any single contributor or institution.
- Showing empathy toward other community members, particularly those whose research contexts differ from the framework's original development context.

Examples of behaviour that fails the standard and will not be tolerated include:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Making citations or empirical claims that the contributor knows or should know to be false; fabricated citations and dishonest scholarship are violations of this code as much as they are violations of the framework's evidentiary discipline.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional academic setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all framework community spaces — including the repository's issue tracker, pull request reviews, and any official discussion forums — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the framework in public spaces. Examples of representing the framework include using an official email address, posting via an official social-media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer through the contact channels listed in `PROVENANCE.md`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

The maintainer will determine the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this code, which may include:

- A private warning with explanation.
- A public clarification and request for behaviour change.
- A temporary or permanent ban from the framework's community spaces.
- Removal of contributions associated with the violator.

## Attribution

This code is adapted from common open-source community standards and from the academic professional-conduct expectations articulated by the Committee on Publication Ethics and major academic societies. It is a working document and will be revised as the community matures.

## Contact for concerns

For concerns that should be raised privately rather than through public channels, contact the maintainer through the affiliations listed in `PROVENANCE.md`. Reports made in good faith will be handled with discretion and timeliness.
